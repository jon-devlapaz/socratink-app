# Socratink north star

## North star

Socratink manages reconstruction evidence and the next useful learning action.

It acts like a knowledge system for demonstrated capability. Sources are
context. Work-relevant capabilities organize the system. Exact learner
attempts supply the evidence.

The AI maintains the model and recommends one action. The learner does the
cognitive work and keeps control of the route.

## Who it is for

Working professionals who must understand and explain technical material for
real work.

The alpha starts with public, personal, or non-confidential technical material.
It does not invite proprietary work material under the current data policy.

## The job

Help me find what I can explain without the source, where my explanation
breaks, and what I should reconstruct next.

## The promise

**Close the source. Explain it. See what survives.**

Socratink preserves each exact attempt. It locates one consequential gap
against the source and asks the learner to repair it. Later, Socratink asks for
another unaided attempt and reports only what the attempts support.

## The durable unit

A **work-relevant capability** is the durable unit.

A capability states what the learner needs to explain or use at work. It is
not a file, chapter, topic label, or AI-generated concept node.

One capability can collect evidence from many sources, attempts, repairs, and
work contexts. When a source overlaps prior work, the AI recommends one of
three actions:

1. Attach the source to an existing capability.
2. Update an existing capability.
3. Create a new capability.

The learner confirms every choice.

## Alpha intake

The first learner inputs are:

1. One pasted or uploaded piece of technical material.
2. One explanation target.

File upload is transport, not the product promise. The AI proposes one focused
capability from the source. The learner confirms or edits it and can add the
work situation where it matters.

The first attempt does not require a course, graph, study plan, or profile
dashboard. The alpha must warn learners to use only public, personal, or
non-confidential material.

## The closed loop

1. The learner adds one source.
2. The AI proposes one work-relevant capability.
3. The learner confirms or edits the capability and its work context.
4. The learner deliberately closes the source. The capability stays visible.
5. The learner writes the first unaided explanation.
6. Socratink preserves the exact attempt.
7. Socratink compares the attempt with the source.
8. Socratink shows one consequential, source-grounded gap.
9. The learner repairs the gap in their own words.
10. The learner leaves for a real interval.
11. Socratink asks for another source-hidden attempt.
12. Socratink compares the exact attempts and recommends the next action.

The learner can reopen the source at any time. Socratink preserves the draft,
marks the attempt as assisted, and excludes it from independent reconstruction
evidence.

SEDA can orchestrate this loop internally. It is not learner-facing navigation.

## Evidence contract

- An eligible source-hidden learner attempt is evidence.
- Socratink preserves the exact attempt unless the learner deletes it.
- The source, capability, work context, confidence, and AI feedback are context.
- A gap and a repair are useful records. They are not independent evidence.
- An assisted attempt is a record. It is not independent evidence.
- Completion, immediate fluency, and AI agreement are not mastery.
- Only eligible reconstruction after real elapsed time can strengthen the
  current capability claim.
- A plausible explanation is not enough. Socratink checks it against the active
  capability and source.
- A route override changes the next action. It does not change historical
  evidence.
- Socratink states uncertainty and never reports more than the attempts support.

## The learning profile

The learning profile is a revisable evidence ledger. It is not a personality
profile, learning-style label, intelligence score, or hidden ability score.

The profile keeps three layers separate:

1. **Learner context:** work goals, constraints, and preferred session length.
2. **Observed evidence:** exact attempts, assistance, recurring gaps, repair
   effects, calibration, and response to spacing.
3. **Adaptive policy:** the next capability, interval, prompt angle, and minimum
   useful scaffold.

One attempt can create a tentative observation. A pattern requires repeated
evidence. Each pattern must show its sample size, recency, assistance
conditions, uncertainty, and counterevidence.

Capability evidence stays capability-specific. Socratink can generalize a
learning-process pattern only after repeated evidence across capabilities.

The learner sees one relevant observation beside the next action. A
**Why this?** control shows the supporting attempts. The learner can edit
personal context and challenge or dismiss an inferred observation.

Socratink can sometimes ask the learner to predict later performance. The
prediction is context. The later source-hidden attempt supplies the evidence.

## AI and interface contract

The AI is an invisible planning system, not the main conversation surface.

- Each screen presents one primary cognitive action.
- The AI removes planning and navigation work. It does not perform the
  reconstruction for the learner.
- No summary, model answer, generated notes, or concept map appears before the
  first attempt.
- The learner can inspect the reason for a recommendation and choose another
  route.
- The detailed model stays below the interface. The learner sees the active
  capability, exact evidence, and next action.

Ink and paper are functional product language:

- sources are documents;
- attempts are preserved ink;
- gaps are precise marginal notes;
- profile observations are field notes; and
- the next action is the next page.

The interface removes operational friction and preserves productive retrieval
difficulty.

## Category boundary

Socratink is **reconstruction practice for working professionals**.

It is not a generic AI tutor, summarizer, flashcard generator, file library,
course platform, or knowledge-graph product. It does not organize documents as
the primary value.

The wedge is exact, free-form, source-hidden explanation compared across time.
This is a positioning advantage, not a proven moat. Trusted longitudinal
evidence and useful next actions must earn any defensibility claim.

## Decision filter

Build a product change only when it improves at least one of these:

- reaching the first unaided attempt;
- keeping the work-relevant capability clear;
- grounding one precise and useful gap;
- enabling a learner-authored repair without answer substitution;
- getting the learner back for delayed reconstruction;
- keeping the evidence durable, truthful, and understandable; or
- selecting a better next action from prior evidence.

Defer graph-first storytelling, a permanent profile dashboard, generic chat,
pre-attempt summaries, generated flashcards, broad ingestion, automatic merges,
fixed learner traits, and engagement features that do not improve the loop.

## North-star behavior

A working professional returns after real elapsed time and attempts the same
capability again without the source.

The later attempt can improve, stay incomplete, or reveal a new gap. Socratink
preserves both attempts, updates only what the evidence supports, and recommends
one next action that the learner can override.

This doctrine is not proof of demand, retention, willingness to pay, or learning
efficacy. The alpha must test whether working professionals:

- reach the first unaided attempt;
- judge the selected gap as source-grounded and consequential;
- write their own repair;
- return for another attempt after real elapsed time;
- trust the record and next action; and
- choose to keep using or pay for the loop.
