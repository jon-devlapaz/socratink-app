# Decision Log Entry

## Date

- `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM TZ`

## Decision

- one-line statement of what was decided

## Context

- the constraint, problem, or ambiguity that required the decision

## Options Considered

- `Option A` — short description
- `Option B` — short description

## Decision Outcome

- what was chosen
- why it won

## Evidence Weighed

- docs, logs, runtime evidence, screenshots, or review findings that informed the call

## Consequences

- what this changes
- what it rules out
- what remains intentionally unresolved

## Follow-Up

- durable doc/code updates required because of this decision
